Our Journey

E-Notes started as a personal project in 2025 to solve the developer's own frustrations with existing note-taking applications. After months of development and testing with close friends, the first public version was launched in early 2025.

What began as a simple note-taking app has evolved into a full-featured productivity platform, thanks to valuable feedback from our growing community of users.

We remain committed to our core principles: simplicity, privacy, and user-focused design.
